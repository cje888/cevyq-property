Buying as a foreigner

Simple answers

Can a foreigner own a condo in Thailand?

Yes. You can own a condo unit outright in your own name, as long as the building's foreign quota is not full. I check the quota before you view.

What about houses and pool villas?

Foreigners cannot own the land, so houses and villas are usually bought on a registered long lease. I explain each option clearly and work with an independent lawyer you choose.

What does renting cost up front?

Normally two months deposit plus the first month's rent, on a one year lease. You pay no agent fee as a tenant.

Can you help from overseas?

Of course. I do live video viewings, send full photo sets and can arrange everything before you land.
