Dec 11: 30 cards

For each card:
  1. In Thunderbird click Write. Copy the To and the Subject (A Professional Card) across.
  2. Click Copy next to Message, click in the Thunderbird message and press Ctrl+V. The picture and the green button open their card.
  3. Send it, then tick Email sent, WhatsApp sent or Text sent, whichever way you sent it. That logs it on dashboard.cevyq.com.
Tip: use open card to look at a card yourself. Your own looks are not counted.
